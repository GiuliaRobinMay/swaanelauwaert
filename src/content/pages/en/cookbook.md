<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>The Taste of Her Journey</em> is much more than a simple cookbook: it is a rich sensory, historical, and spiritual exploration. Through 100 creative recipes and evocative stories, Swaane Lauwaert retraces Mary Magdalene’s legendary path—from the sun-drenched shores of Galilee to the serene silence of the Sainte-Baume massif in Provence.</p>
