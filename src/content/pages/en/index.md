<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. Coaching, Bodywork &amp; Intimacy:</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Sometimes, daily rush, old patterns, or accumulated tension in the nervous system disconnect us from our own foundation and inner center. In my 1:1 sessions, I blend body-oriented coaching, somatic bodywork, and nervous system regulation.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A safe, non-judgmental space to truly land back in your body and feel what is alive within you. While I specialize in desire, intimacy, and female sexuality, this is never a requirement. Whatever you are navigating right now—whether you are looking for deep rest, facing a transition, or simply longing to feel more grounded—is always our starting point.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. Mary Magdalena retreat South of France 2027</strong>:</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A deeply immersive sanctuary under the warm southern French sun, designed to let you gently land back home in yourself. While the exact details are still unfolding, this retreat will weave the sacred ancient forest of the Sainte-Baume and the archetypal energy of Mary Magdalene into an invitation to soften, open, and reconnect with your body. Envision somatic bodywork, genuine nervous system rest, nourishing shared meals, and mindful walks through this potent, timeless landscape. No rushing or crowded schedules—simply a grounded, intimate space where nothing is forced and everything is welcome to unfold naturally.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3. Cookbook &amp; Mary Magdalene: The Taste of HER Journey</strong>:</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This book is written for the intuitive cook, the seeker of deeper meaning, and the storyteller. For anyone who knows that a meal is so much more than just fuel and sustenance. It is about memories, rituals, and our connection to the earth. Above all, it is about love: the simple magic of eating together, welcoming people around your table, and celebrating life side by side. In my debut book, <em>The Taste of Her Journey</em>, I invite you into my kitchen and into the places that move me deeply—including the ancient, sacred land of Mary Magdalene. The result is a collection of stories and recipes that warm the heart and bring us together.</p>
