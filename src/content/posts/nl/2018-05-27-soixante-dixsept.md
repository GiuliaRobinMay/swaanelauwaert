<p>Carrying the dream.</p>
<p>Olav H. Hauge‘s<br />
(Translated by Robin Fulton)</p>
<p>It’s the dream we carry in secret<br />
that something miraculous will happen,<br />
that it must happen –<br />
that time will open<br />
that the heart will open<br />
that doors will open<br />
that the mountains will open<br />
that springs will gush –<br />
that the dream will open,<br />
that one morning we will glide into<br />
some little harbour we didn’t know was there.</p>
